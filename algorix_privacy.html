Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Scientific Calc ("the app") is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy explains what information the app does, and does not, collect.

Data Collection

Scientific Calc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and does not connect to any external server.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any information the app uses, such as calculation history, saved matrices, and graphed equations, is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Third-Party Services

Scientific Calc does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Scientific Calc does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a new feature requires data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@yourdomain.com.
